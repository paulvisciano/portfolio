Where is Paul?

A globe and a timeline. A travel record as a place you can move through. Not a feed. Not a photo dump.

Where is Paul? is a spatial life map. A globe. A timeline. Stories that turn real moments into nodes you can return to. Places, people, and time are first-class.

The live map is the public demo. Your copy is the one that runs on your machine, from your own export.

What it is

  • Globe + timeline. Move through years and places as one surface.
  • Stories as structure. Moments become nodes you can return to.
  • Web-first. Designed for the browser. The same spatial grammar is meant for XR.

Live exploration sits on the work grid and at paulvisciano.com/apps/where-is-paul. Paste a prompt, drop a Takeout, get a globe with your name on it: One prompt. Your own life map.
