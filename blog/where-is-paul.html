Where is Paul?

A living spatial record of a life — globe, timeline, and stories that turn real moments into something navigable.

Where is Paul? is a spatial life map: a globe, a timeline, and stories that turn real moments into something you can travel through. Places, people, and time are first-class — not a feed, not a photo dump.

A Sci-Fi app for the record of a life — navigable in space and time.

What it is

  • Globe + timeline — move through years and places as a continuous surface
  • Stories as structure — moments become nodes you can return to
  • Web-first — designed for the browser, with the same spatial grammar meant for XR

Live exploration and archive links sit on the portfolio work grid and at paulvisciano.com/apps/where-is-paul.
