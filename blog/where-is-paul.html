Where is Paul?

A spatial web app. A life on a globe. Not a feed. Not a photo dump. A place you move through.

He was bouncing the globe as a digital nomad. Family kept asking where Paul was. The answer is a globe. Cities, crossings, stays. Comics and photos on the pins. Time as a surface you can scrub.

This is a spatial web app. It runs in the browser first. Same URL on a phone, a desk, and Safari on Vision Pro. Pinch to zoom. Look to look around. No app store. No extra session.

https://paulvisciano.com/apps/where-is-paul/

The live map is the demo. Your copy is a clone. The only file you change is moments/moments.js.

How to read the globe

  • Hex color is stay duration. How long you were there. Not height. Longer stay, different color.
  • Globe + timeline. Move through years and places as one surface. Stories sit on the path.
  • Mixed pins. Posts, cards, short video, comic sequences from real photographs.

Same URL, including the headset

On a phone it is the page. On a desk it is the page. On Vision Pro it is still the page, opened in Safari. Pinch to zoom. Look to look around. There is no separate build. Spatial apps are web apps.

Make your own

Clone github.com/paulvisciano/where-is-paul. Edit moments/moments.js. Date, place, stay, media. That is the fork door.
