Musical Cubes

Music production as something you turn — rotating 3D instruments on web, PWA, and native mobile.

Musical Cubes reimagines the timeline DAW as a set of tactile 3D cubes. Each cube is a track; each face is an instrument stem. Rotate to solo, mute, or rearrange. The same spatial language works on a phone in your hand and a browser on a desk.

Music Production Reimagined — a visual, physical alternative to endless horizontal timelines.

What it is

  • 3D track cubes — rotate to switch stems and hear the mix change in real time
  • Web + PWA + native — Ionic, React, Capacitor; one product surface across platforms
  • Spatial first — production as something you manipulate in space, not only a list of channels

Live at musical-cubes.vercel.app.
