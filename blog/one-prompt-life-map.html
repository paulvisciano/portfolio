The record already exists

If you used Google Maps or had location services on, Google saved a detailed record of where you went. Not only the big trips. Coffee shops. Routes to work. Weekends away. It went into Timeline, sometimes still labeled Location History.

A lot of people never looked at it. For years it was on by default for many accounts. Google later moved a lot of that history onto the phone itself. The data is yours. Most of it still sits inside their world instead of becoming a place you can walk.

This is the practical side of reclaiming your data. Get the record out. Put it on a globe you control.

What the map is

Where is Paul? takes real location history and turns it into an interactive globe and timeline. You spin the world. You scrub through time. You click a place. The shape of a life in motion.

The same idea works for anyone. You do not need to be a developer. You need two things: your own Google Maps Timeline export, and one short prompt that tells an AI to use the live site as a template.

Copy the prompt. Paste it. When the app is ready, drop in your Timeline file. That is the whole process.

The prompt

Copy everything in the box below and paste it into Grok, Claude, ChatGPT, or any AI that can build web apps.

Build me a personal life map like this one: https://paulvisciano.com/apps/where-is-paul

Use the live site as the template. Make it mine — rename it to “Where is [My Name]?” and set it up so I can drop in my own Google Maps Timeline export and have the globe and timeline fill with my places automatically.

That is it. The AI uses the public site as the model and builds a version that runs on your data.

How to get your Google Maps Timeline data

Google changed how this works. A few years ago the data mostly lived on their servers. Starting in late 2024 they moved a lot of it onto the phone itself. So there are two places to look.

1. From your phone (recent trips)

Android

Open your phone's Settings app, then go to:

Location → Location services → Timeline

You will see a screen like this:

  1. Tap Export Timeline data
  2. Tap Continue and choose where to save the file
  3. You will get a file named Timeline.json

iPhone

  • Open the Google Maps app
  • Tap your profile picture (top right)
  • Go to Settings → Personal content (or Location & Privacy)
  • Tap Export Timeline data → Save to Files

2. From Google Takeout (older history)

  1. Go to takeout.google.com and sign in.
  2. Click “Deselect all.”
  3. Find and check only Location History (Timeline).
  4. Choose JSON and create the export.
  5. Google will email you a download link. Unzip the file when it arrives.

If you want the fullest picture, do both. Older data often still lives in Takeout. Newer data is usually on the device.

Where it runs

Your data stays on your machine if you run the finished app locally. The public Where is Paul? site is the example. The version built from your own Timeline is the one that belongs to you. Offline is the proof.
