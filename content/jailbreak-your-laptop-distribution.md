## The Rule

The opener (`own-your-hardware.html`) makes the case. The five parts show the how. Every channel points at the same five parts:

1. PWN the OS
2. Run Your Local AI
3. Create Your Own Apps
4. Create Your Own Site — the public layer
5. Publish If You Want. Reclaim Your Data.

The PDF is the canonical artifact. The opener sells the thesis. Blogs expand each part. Videos show it live. Shorts are the hook. The recipe never changes — only the container does.
