About

Now I design and build the full stack myself — web, mobile, and XR — using AI as a collaborator. Through Sci-Fi Labs the goal is the same: turn science-fiction ideas into real products, and push web technologies past their usual limits.

Underneath it all is a longer bet. Sovereignty. Memory you own. Models you run. Tools that answer to you — not rented, not watched, public only when you choose.

Before going independent, I was a Principal Engineer at OutSystems — the AI-powered low-code platform used by more than a thousand enterprises worldwide. I moved there specifically to work closer with the Ionic team after the acquisition, and did: design systems, shared UI, and the patterns teams reuse so enterprise software doesn't reinvent itself on every project. That work crossed into Stencil.js. I partnered with Apple to implement features for their internal design system built on Stencil, and contributed to the open-source project directly.

Before that, more than a decade at Paylocity on Core Mobile — the app employees open to clock in, request time off, and check a paycheck. Paylocity is a leading cloud HCM platform serving roughly 44,400 companies and millions of people. We rebuilt the mobile stack more than once on Ionic and Xamarin so other teams could ship without fighting the platform.
