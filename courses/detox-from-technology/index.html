TAKEAWAYA notification audit and your first quiet hour.

TAKEAWAYA calmer home screen, built around what you value.

Use a private, handwritten habit log to notice the cue, the urge, and the need underneath. Start with observation rather than a target to hit.

TAKEAWAYA personal map of your checking habits.

TAKEAWAYA small daily presence ritual you can repeat.

Notice what keeps you scrolling, set an intention before opening an app, and decide in advance when you will leave.

TAKEAWAYA social media routine with a beginning and an end.

TAKEAWAYA realistic daily rhythm, with room to adjust.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Room for questions.

How do I sign up for the course?

Join the waitlist above to hear when enrollment opens. We’ll send the course details and explain how to sign up. Joining the waitlist is free and does not enroll you in a paid course.

Who is this for?

People who want to spend less time checking and scrolling, and more time choosing where their attention goes. The practices are designed to fit around everyday work and responsibilities.

Will I have to give up my phone?

The focus is intentional use: keep the technology that supports your life, and practise boundaries around the habits that pull you away. You choose the changes that work for you.

How long is it, and what will it cost?

The course follows eight parts, from managing notifications to building sustainable digital habits. Join the waitlist for the format, schedule, and price when enrollment opens. There’s no payment to join the waitlist.

What happens after I join?

Your email is added to the course waitlist so Paul can send enrollment details and course updates. You won’t receive an automated confirmation email. You can ask to leave the list at any time through the contact link in our privacy details.
